Questions?

Frequently Asked

What's included in web development projects?
▼
Every project includes: custom design, responsive mobile layout, SEO optimization, and fast loading performance. I deliver clean, hand-coded HTML/CSS/JavaScript (or React for complex apps). All projects come with 1 month of free maintenance and unlimited revisions during development.
How long does a typical project take?
▼
Landing pages take 2–3 days, full websites take 5–7 days, and web apps take 2–3 weeks depending on complexity. I also offer express delivery (rush turnaround) for urgent projects. We can discuss your timeline during the consultation.
Can I request changes after delivery?
▼
Absolutely! Every project includes unlimited revisions during development and 1 month of free post-launch support. After that, I offer ongoing maintenance packages at competitive hourly rates. Regular backups and updates are included in the support period.
Do you provide hosting and domain help?
▼
I can help you set up hosting (I recommend Vercel, Netlify, or DigitalOcean for best performance). Domain registration and DNS setup are also handled. I can either set it up for you or guide you through the process — your choice.
What about payment terms and deposits?
▼
I require a 50% deposit upfront to start the project, and the remaining 50% is due upon delivery. This ensures commitment from both sides. I accept UPI, bank transfer, or online payment methods. For long-term contracts, we can discuss custom payment schedules.
Can you help with existing websites or migration?
▼
Yes! I offer website redesigns, performance optimization, security audits, and migration from old platforms. If your current site is slow or outdated, I can rebuild it with modern technologies while preserving your content and SEO value. Let's chat about your specific needs.
What about Python automation on my Mac/Linux?
▼
Python tools work on Windows, Mac, and Linux. For executables (.exe), those are Windows-specific, but I can deliver cross-platform solutions using Python scripts or web-based automation dashboards. Just let me know your OS preferences in the initial consultation.
Do you offer ongoing retainer support?
▼
Definitely! I offer monthly retainer packages starting at ₹8,000/month for ongoing support, bug fixes, feature updates, and optimization. This is perfect for businesses that need consistent improvements and new features. Custom retainer plans available based on your needs.
