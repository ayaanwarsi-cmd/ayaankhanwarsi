Frequently Asked Questions

How much does an e-commerce website cost?+

E-commerce platform costs range from ₹100,000 for basic stores to ₹500,000+ for feature-rich platforms. Costs depend on number of products, integrations, customization, and payment methods. Get detailed quotes based on specific requirements.

What payment methods should I support?+

Support multiple payment options: credit/debit cards, digital wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay), UPI, net banking, and cash on delivery. Each customer segment has preferences; offering choices increases conversion rates.

How important is mobile optimization?+

Mobile shopping dominates e-commerce. Over 70% of traffic typically comes from mobile. Mobile optimization isn't optional—it's essential. Poor mobile experience loses customers to competitors.

Can you integrate with my existing systems?+

Yes. E-commerce platforms integrate with suppliers, logistics systems, accounting software, CRM systems, and marketing tools. Proper integration eliminates manual data entry and improves efficiency.

What security measures are needed?+

E-commerce requires PCI DSS compliance, SSL encryption, secure payment processing, fraud detection, secure customer data storage, and regular security audits. Security is non-negotiable for customer trust.
