Lead Generation System

Automated Lead Capture and Nurturing Platform

Project Overview

Lead Generation System is a comprehensive platform for capturing, tracking, and nurturing sales leads through automated workflows. Built for a B2B marketing agency, this system integrates lead capture from multiple sources, CRM functionality, email marketing automation, and analytics. The platform has enabled the agency to scale operations, improve lead quality, and increase conversion rates dramatically. The system processes thousands of leads monthly with automated qualification and nurturing.

This project demonstrates expertise in complex automation, API integrations, database design for high-volume data, and building marketing technology solutions. The platform has proven instrumental in the client's business growth and operational efficiency.

Problem Statement

Client Challenges

Manual lead entry and qualification consuming excessive staff time. Leads captured from multiple sources but not centralized. No systematic follow-up process leading to missed opportunities. Limited visibility into lead quality and conversion rates. Difficulty scaling operations as lead volume increased. Email follow-up inconsistent and manual.

Business Impact

Low conversion rates due to poor lead follow-up. Wasted marketing spend on low-quality leads. Sales team inefficient with scattered lead information. Difficulty scaling without proportional staff increase. Lost opportunities due to delayed responses.

Solution Architecture

Lead Capture System

Web forms for multiple lead sources with smart form logic. Website chat integration capturing visitor information. CTA buttons and landing page forms. API endpoints for third-party integrations. Mobile-responsive forms for all devices.

Lead Management Platform

Centralized lead database with detailed profiles. Lead scoring based on engagement and data. Qualification workflow automating lead assessment. Lead routing to appropriate sales team members. CRM functionality for lead tracking.

Automation Engine

Workflow builder for custom lead nurturing sequences. Email automation with personalization tokens. Task assignment and reminders for follow-up. Conditional logic for dynamic workflows. Integration with calendar and meeting scheduling.

Analytics Dashboard

Real-time lead metrics and KPIs. Conversion funnel analysis. Campaign performance tracking. Lead quality analysis. ROI calculation by source.

Key Features Implemented

Multi-Channel Lead Capture

Web forms with conditional logic. Chat widget capturing visitor information. Landing page builder. Email list import functionality. API for programmatic lead submission.

Lead Scoring and Qualification

Automated scoring based on engagement metrics. Behavioral tracking and analysis. Lead qualification rules. Automatic qualification workflow. Lead routing to sales.

Email Automation

Drip email campaigns. Personalized email content. Automated follow-up sequences. Email timing optimization. A/B testing for subject lines and content.

CRM Integration

Two-way sync with popular CRM systems. Contact management and history. Activity tracking and notes. Sales pipeline management. Forecasting and reporting.

Technologies Used

Frontend

React.js for dashboard and forms. Chart libraries for analytics. Form builder using drag-and-drop. API communication with Axios.

Backend

Node.js Express for API server. Queue systems for async processing. Webhook handlers for integrations. Email service integration. Third-party API integrations.

Database

PostgreSQL for primary data store. MongoDB for flexible lead data. Redis for caching and queue management. Elasticsearch for lead search.

Integrations

Email service provider (SendGrid/AWS SES). CRM systems (Salesforce, HubSpot). Calendar services (Google Calendar). Payment processors. Analytics platforms.
